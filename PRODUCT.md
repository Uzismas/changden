# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Anyone who needs a house roof repainted: mainly homeowners, but also anyone hiring on someone's behalf. They find the site through Google search or Facebook, check real work and process, then contact the team through LINE or phone. Visitors use every device class: phone, tablet/iPad, and desktop PC; all must work fully.

## Product Purpose

Marketing and lead-generation site for ช่างเด่น (Chang Den), a roof-painting team. The site exists to make a visitor confident enough to send roof photos on LINE or call for a free on-site assessment. Success = LINE contacts and phone calls.

## Positioning

A team that does one thing: spray-painting and repainting house roofs (tile and metal sheet). Free on-site assessment, step-by-step surface preparation, and real before/after work from its own jobs (Ban Don, Na Khwan, Rayong). Strong in the Eastern region (Rayong, Chonburi, Pattaya) plus Bangkok and vicinity.

## Operating Context

Visitors compare several roof painters before choosing. They want to see real results, understand the process, and contact the team with as little effort as possible (LINE is the primary channel, phone second). Thai-language site.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML/CSS/JS site deployed on Vercel; hand-written stylesheet at `assets/site.css`; `site.js` for the mobile menu and small behaviors.
- Single service only: house roof spray-painting / repainting (tile and metal sheet). No waterproofing, leak repair, ceramic heat coating, factory, warehouse, or building work.
- Prices are never shown on the site; visitors ask via LINE or phone.
- SEO content and page structure (titles, headings, FAQ, schema, internal links) were written on 2026-11-08 and must be preserved through any redesign.
- Service area: Rayong, Chonburi, Pattaya, Bangkok, Nonthaburi, Pathum Thani, Samut Prakan, Samut Sakhon.
